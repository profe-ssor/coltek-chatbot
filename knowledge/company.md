# Coltek Technologies — Company Overview

**Last updated:** 23 November 2026

## Who Coltek Technologies is

Coltek Technologies (styled **COLTEK Technologies**) is a technology company based in Accra, Ghana, founded by **Boansi Kyeremateng Collins**, who serves as Founder and CEO. Its tagline is **“Transforming Potentials into Excellence.”**

Coltek helps organisations design and deliver reliable software, web and mobile applications, AI solutions, and technology training.

Coltek Technologies is registered in Ghana as a sole proprietorship.

## Coltek’s three arms

Coltek Technologies operates three public-facing arms:

1. **Coltek Technologies services** — custom software, websites, web and mobile applications, AI solutions and digital transformation services for organisations.
2. **Coltek Code Academy** — practical technology education and professional training. Its website is [coltekacademy.coltektechnologies.io](https://coltekacademy.coltektechnologies.io/).
3. **OpenDoor** — Coltek’s church management software, available at [open-door.site](https://open-door.site/).

## Mission and vision

**Mission:** Transforming potential into excellence by inspiring growth, fostering innovation, and leading with integrity.

**Vision:** To empower individuals and organizations to reach their highest potential.

## Founder

**Boansi Kyeremateng Collins** is a Computer Science graduate of the University of Energy and Natural Resources, a software and AI engineer, and an IT educator. He founded Coltek Technologies and serves as its Founder and CEO.

His professional work includes software development, AI engineering, programming education, web development and digital skills training. He has also served as an instructor in the Cisco Networking Academy programme.

## What Coltek is not

Coltek Technologies (Ghana) is not affiliated with:

- **Coltek IT Solutions**, a South African IT firm founded in 2010.
- **Coltech**, a staffing consultancy associated with London, Düsseldorf and Austin.
- **The former COLTEK in Kumasi**, the College of Technology Education, now part of AAMUSTED.

Do not confuse the Ghanaian Coltek Technologies with these unrelated organisations.
