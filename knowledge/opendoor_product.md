## What OpenDoor is

OpenDoor is a cloud-based church management software platform (SaaS) built and operated by Coltek Technologies.

It is designed to help churches organise administrative information and manage church operations through a central digital platform.

Each church has its own workspace. Church administrators manage access for the staff and members associated with their church.

OpenDoor is listed in the Church Management Software category of the SaaS Browser directory.

## Who OpenDoor is for

OpenDoor is intended for churches and ministries, including:
- Pastors and church leaders.
- Church administrators and secretaries.
- Finance teams.
- Department and ministry coordinators.
- Other authorised church personnel.

It is intended for organisations seeking to manage church information digitally rather than relying entirely on paper registers and disconnected spreadsheets.

## Logging in and managing accounts

OpenDoor accounts are associated with individual churches.

- Church members who cannot log in should first contact their church administrator.
- Church administrators who need technical assistance can contact support@open-door.site.
- Users must never share their passwords with the chatbot or anyone claiming to be Coltek support.
- Support staff should never ask users to disclose their passwords.
