### Placement assessment

Applicants may be asked to take Coltek’s General IT Knowledge and Skills Assessment. The assessment is expected to take approximately 45–60 minutes and may include written questions, practical exercises and a short interview.

The assessment is intended to identify the learner’s current level and suitable starting point. It should not be described as pass-or-fail unless the Academy formally changes the policy.

## How enrolment works

The current proposed enrolment description is:

1. Learners register through the Academy website.
2. Learners receive a confirmation email from Coltek Code Academy.
3. Enrolled learners access course materials through their student dashboard.
4. Learners may be invited to the relevant student WhatsApp community for announcements.
